ATAG Zone for Homey

Read your boiler and room thermostat through the remotethermo platform, the same platform the ATAG Zone phone app uses.

What you get in Homey:
- Room temperature and target temperature
- Outdoor temperature
- Central heating water pressure
- Whether the burner is firing

Flow cards:
- When the boiler starts or stops firing
- When water pressure drops below a value you choose, so you notice in time

Brands:
You pick your brand when adding the device. ATAG Zone and Ariston NET have been tested against a real installation. Chaffoteaux and ELCO use the same platform and are selectable, but untested.

Safe by default:
The app only reads. Setting the temperature and mode is built in but stays off until you enable it in the app settings, so nothing changes to your heating unless you want it to.
